Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

nook: offline games & puzzles is built as an offline-first sanctuary. We believe your digital downtime should belong entirely to you. This Privacy Policy details our handling of user data.

1. No Data Collection

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, device identifiers, locations, or usage analytics. When you play, no data leaves your device.

2. 100% Offline Architecture

All game progress, personal settings, and puzzle states are saved solely on your local device storage using standard browser/webview APIs. We maintain no servers, user accounts, or external databases.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

The application contains no advertisements, tracking cookies, or third-party analytical software development kits (SDKs).

4. Children’s Privacy

Because our application collects zero personal information from any user, it is fully compliant with global children's privacy standards, including COPPA and GDPR-K.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this policy, you may contact the developer through our official Google Play developer listing page.
